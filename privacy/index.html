Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026
This policy describes the current local/private beta version of Clip Relay. Replace the placeholder contact email before publishing this page.

1. Overview

Clip Relay is a desktop publishing companion. It is designed so that most application data remains on the user's own computer rather than in a central Clip Relay account database.

2. Information Clip Relay handles

Depending on the platforms you connect and the features you use, Clip Relay may handle:

  • connected-account identifiers such as channel or profile IDs, display names, avatars, and account nicknames;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens supplied by connected platforms after you authorize Clip Relay;
  • video files, titles, descriptions/captions, privacy choices, scheduling information, and publishing history;
  • local application settings, queue state, and technical logs needed to operate or troubleshoot the app.

3. Passwords

Clip Relay does not ask for or store the passwords for your YouTube/Google, TikTok, Instagram, or other connected social accounts. Account authorization takes place through the destination platform's own sign-in and OAuth process.

4. Local storage and token protection

Clip Relay stores its application data locally on the user's device. Sensitive authorization credentials are stored using Electron's operating-system-backed secure storage where available, rather than being intentionally stored as readable plain-text passwords.

5. Third-party services

When you connect or publish to a platform, Clip Relay communicates with that platform's APIs. The destination service receives information required to authenticate the account and publish the content you requested. Their own privacy policies govern their processing of that information.

6. Temporary media transfer

Some publishing workflows may require a temporary publicly reachable media URL. For example, Instagram publishing can require the destination service to fetch a video from a hosted URL. If you configure Clip Relay to use temporary object storage such as Cloudflare R2, the selected media may be uploaded there for the publishing operation and removed afterward according to the app's configured cleanup process.

7. Selling or advertising use of data

Clip Relay does not sell personal information or connected-account data. The current private beta does not use your connected-account information for targeted advertising.

8. Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect accounts in Clip Relay and remove locally stored account connections. You can also remove local publishing history and application data by using the application's controls or uninstalling/resetting the local application data. Revoking a platform authorization from the platform itself may also invalidate previously issued tokens.

9. Security

No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Windows account and device protected, install updates, and revoke a connected platform session if you believe your device or tokens may have been compromised.

10. Children's privacy

Clip Relay is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The current private beta is intended for use by its authorized adult operator.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated as Clip Relay's architecture or connected services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
